## What they asked for
Traders want Maize in the commodity list.

## Decision
Corn and Maize are the same crop, so Corn is renamed to Maize
instead of adding a second row. Confirmed by Yermal Udaya Shreesha on 2026-10-06.

## Affects existing
The existing Corn commodity is renamed to Maize. Trades that used
Corn keep pointing to the same commodity and now show "Maize".

## Out of scope
Letting users add commodities themselves (BR-9 stays as it is).
