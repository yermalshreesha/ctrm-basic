# Change: Clear filters button on the Trades list
Requested by: Yermal Udaya Shreesha   Date: [today's date]
Status: in progress

## What they asked for
After filtering the Trades list, resetting means changing each
dropdown back to "All" by hand. Users want one button to clear them.

## Details
- A "Clear filters" button next to the filters on the Trades list.
- Clicking it resets status, commodity and direction to "All", and
  shows all trades again.
- The button only shows when at least one filter is active.
- Follows the look-and-feel rules (rule 9 in ctrm-standards).

## Affects existing
Only the Trades list screen. No backend or data changes.

## Out of scope
The same button on the Logistics list (could be a later change).
